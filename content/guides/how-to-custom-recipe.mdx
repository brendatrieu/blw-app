summary: Save something you make at home, so you can log it, store it, and find it again in a tap.
---

Your own recipes sit right next to ours. Once one is saved, you can log it as a meal or add a batch to storage from its page.

## 1. Tap Add recipe

Open **Recipes** in the bottom bar, then tap **Add recipe** at the top.

![The top of the Recipes page, with the Add recipe button at the top right.](/guides/recipe-1-add.webp)

## 2. Name it and pick an age

Type a **Title**. Under **Suitable from**, choose the youngest age it suits.

![The Title field reads Sweet Potato Lentil Patties, and Suitable from is set to 8 months.](/guides/recipe-2-title-age.webp)

## 3. Add the foods in it

Tap **Ingredients** and type to search. Tap each food to check it, then tap **Done**. The recipe's iron, fiber, and allergen tags come from these foods.

![The Ingredients search shows "potato", with Sweet Potato checked and a Done button below.](/guides/recipe-3-ingredients.webp)

## 4. Add amounts and extras (optional)

Each food gets its own quantity field, like "half a cup, cooked." Use **Additional ingredients** for things with no food of their own, like oil or water. Tap **+ Add ingredient** for another one.

![Sweet Potato and Lentils each have a quantity filled in, and olive oil is listed under Additional ingredients.](/guides/recipe-4-amounts.webp)

## 5. Write the steps (optional)

Type the first step, then tap **Add step** for the next one. Add a **Prep time** in minutes if you like.

![Two numbered steps are filled in, with the Add step button below and Prep time set to 25.](/guides/recipe-5-steps.webp)

## 6. Tap Save

**Save** is at the bottom of the form. Your recipe opens on its own page, marked **Custom**, with **Log meal** and **Add to storage** ready to go. Find it again anytime under **Recipes**, then **Custom**.

![The new recipe's page shows its tags, Custom among them, the Log meal and Add to storage buttons, its ingredients and its steps.](/guides/recipe-6-saved.webp)
