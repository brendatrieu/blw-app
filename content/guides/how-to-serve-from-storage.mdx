Serving from storage does two jobs at once: it logs the meal and counts down what's left.

## 1. Set the servings and time

Use **−** and **+** to set how many servings your baby had. **When** defaults to the current time. If you served earlier, tap it, scroll to the day and time, and tap **Done**.

![On the plate, Sweet Potato Lentil Patties from the Freezer, 8 of 8 left, has servings set to 1, and When below shows today at 11:59 AM.](/guides/serve-1-servings.webp)

## 2. Serve more than one item (optional)

Tap **+ Add more** to serve something else with it: another item from storage, or a fresh food or recipe that isn't in storage.

![On the plate, Sweet Potato Lentil Patties and a Broccoli, Carrot container each have their own servings, and a fresh Banana is added.](/guides/serve-2-more.webp)

## 3. Rate it with stars (optional)

Each item on the plate has its own rating, and each item within a container has its own rating. Tap one to five stars, five for a big hit. Tap the same star again to clear it.

![Sweet Potato Lentil Patties has four stars, and the Broccoli, Carrot container has a row of stars for each food.](/guides/serve-3-stars.webp)

## 4. Add a reaction or a note (optional)

Use **Reaction** only for signs like hives, vomiting, or a rash. Everything else goes in **Notes**.

![Reaction (optional) is left empty, and Notes reads "Ate half, then played with the rest."](/guides/serve-4-notes.webp)

## 5. Tap Serve

The servings left go down, and the meal lands in your **Food log** marked **From storage**. Served by mistake? Delete the meal from your **Food log**, and the servings go back. Served a different amount? Edit the meal and change its servings.

![The storage item now reads 7 of 8 servings left.](/guides/serve-5-served.webp)
