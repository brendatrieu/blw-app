Serving from storage does two jobs at once: it logs the meal and counts down what's left.

## 1. Set the servings and time

Use **−** and **+** to set how many **Servings** your baby had. **When** starts at right now. Tap it if you served earlier.

![The Serve sheet for Sweet Potato Lentil Patties, with Servings at 1 and When showing today at 11:59 AM.](/guides/serve-1-servings.webp)

## 2. Rate it with stars (optional)

Tap one to five stars for each food, five for a big hit. Tap **Clear** to take a rating back. When you serve a recipe, you rate the recipe once.

![Under Rating (optional), Sweet Potato Lentil Patties has four stars, with a Clear link.](/guides/serve-2-stars.webp)

## 3. Add a reaction or a note

Just like logging a meal, use **Reaction** only for signs like hives, vomiting, or a rash. Everything else goes in **Note**.

![Reaction (optional) is left empty, and Note reads "Ate half, then played with the rest."](/guides/serve-3-notes.webp)

## 4. Tap Serve

The servings left go down, and the meal lands in your **Food log** marked **From storage**. When the last serving is gone, the item moves off your active list.

![The storage item now reads 7 of 8 servings left.](/guides/serve-4-served.webp)
