Logging after each meal builds a picture of what your baby has tried. If a reaction ever shows up, you can trace it back to what they ate.

## 1. Pick the foods

Tap **Food** and type to search. Select each food your baby had, then tap **Done**. Made one or more recipes? Pick them under **Recipes (optional)** and their ingredients fill in for you.

![The Food search shows "avo", with Avocado checked and "2 selected" in the field.](/guides/meal-1-foods.webp)

## 2. Set the time

**When** defaults to the current time. If the meal was earlier, tap it, scroll to the day and time, and tap **Done**.

![The Time picker, set to Today at 11:56 AM, with a Done button at the bottom.](/guides/meal-2-when.webp)

## 3. Rate it with stars (optional)

Tap one to five stars, five for a big hit.

![Under Rating (optional), Banana has three stars and Avocado has five, each with a Clear link.](/guides/meal-3-stars.webp)

## 4. Add a reaction or a note (optional)

Use **Reaction** only for signs like hives, vomiting, or a rash. A reaction note pauses progress on that meal's allergens, so keep likes and dislikes in **Notes**.

![Reaction (optional) is left empty, and Notes reads "Loved the avocado, squished the banana."](/guides/meal-4-notes.webp)

## 5. Tap Save

Tap **Save** at the bottom. The meal shows up in **Food log** on Home. Have leftovers? Turn on **Save leftovers to storage** before you save.

![Home's Food log shows the new meal: Avocado, Banana, today at 11:56 AM, with its note.](/guides/meal-5-saved.webp)
