Logging after each meal builds a picture of what your baby has tried. If a reaction ever shows up, you can trace it back to what they ate.

## 1. Add what your baby had

Tap **+ Add more** and type to search, or look under **In storage**, **Recipes**, and **Foods**. Tick everything your baby had, then tap **Done**. Left something out of a recipe? Tap **Ingredients** under it and untick that food.

![Add more with "avo" typed in the search and Foods picked, and Avocado ticked.](/guides/meal-1-foods.webp)

## 2. Rate it with stars (optional)

Each item on the plate has its own rating. Tap one to five stars, five for a big hit. Tap the same star again to clear it.

![On the plate, Avocado has five stars and Banana has three.](/guides/meal-2-stars.webp)

## 3. Set the time

**When** defaults to the current time. If the meal was earlier, tap it, scroll to the day and time, and tap **Done**.

![The Time picker, set to Today at 11:56 AM, with a Done button at the bottom.](/guides/meal-3-when.webp)

## 4. Add a reaction or a note (optional)

Use **Reaction** only for signs like hives, vomiting, or a rash. A reaction note pauses progress on that meal's allergens, so keep likes and dislikes in **Notes**.

![Reaction (optional) is left empty, and Notes reads "Loved the avocado, squished the banana."](/guides/meal-4-notes.webp)

## 5. Tap Save

Tap **Save** at the bottom. The meal shows up in **Food log** on Home. Have leftovers? Turn on **Save leftovers to storage** before you save. You can edit or delete a meal later. Deleting one that was served from storage gives its servings back.

![Home's Food log shows the new meal, today at 11:56 AM: Avocado rated 5 stars and Banana rated 3, each on its own line, with its note below.](/guides/meal-5-saved.webp)
