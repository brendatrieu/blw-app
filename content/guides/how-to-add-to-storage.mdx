Each item in Storage shows how long it keeps, so nothing gets forgotten at the back of the freezer.

## 1. Tap Add to storage

On **Home**, tap **Add to storage**. You can also tap **+ Add item** on the **Storage** page.

![Home's two big buttons, with Add to storage on the right.](/guides/meal-1-open.webp)

## 2. Choose what you're storing

Tap **From a food**, **From a recipe**, or **Free-form** to type any name. Search and tap your pick. The recipe list closes by itself. For foods you can pick several, then tap **Done**.

![From a recipe is selected, and the Recipe search shows Sweet Potato Lentil Patties.](/guides/storage-2-what.webp)

## 3. Pick where it goes

Tap **Fridge**, **Freezer**, or **Counter**. **Prepared** starts at right now. Tap it if you made the batch earlier.

![Location is set to Freezer, and Prepared shows today at 11:58 AM.](/guides/storage-3-where.webp)

## 4. Add amounts (optional)

A **Quantity note** like "8 patties" helps you remember what's there. Enter **Total servings** and the app counts down each time you serve. Set a **Best by** date if you have one, like from a package. It replaces the usual freshness window.

![Quantity note reads 8 patties, Total servings is 8, and Best by shows Select a date.](/guides/storage-4-amounts.webp)

## 5. Tap Add to storage

The button is at the bottom of the form. You go back to where you started, and your item is listed on the **Storage** page with the servings left and how long it keeps.

![The Storage page lists Sweet Potato Lentil Patties in the Freezer, 8 of 8 servings left, Use within 90d.](/guides/storage-5-saved.webp)
