Each item in Storage shows how long it keeps, so nothing gets forgotten at the back of the freezer.

## 1. Choose what you're storing

Pick what you're storing: a food or a recipe. Search and tap your pick, or several foods for a mixed container. If a food isn't in the list, the search offers to add it as a custom food.

![From a recipe is selected, and the Recipe search shows Sweet Potato Lentil Patties.](/guides/storage-1-what.webp)

## 2. Pick where it goes

Tap **Fridge**, **Freezer**, or **Counter**. Change the prepared time if you made it earlier.

![Location is set to Freezer, and Prepared shows today at 11:58 AM.](/guides/storage-2-where.webp)

## 3. Add amounts (optional)

A **Quantity note** like "8 patties" helps you remember what's there. Enter **Total servings** and the app counts down each time you serve. Set a **Best by** date if you have one, like from a package. It replaces the usual freshness window.

![Quantity note reads 8 patties, Total servings is 8, and Best by shows Select a date.](/guides/storage-3-amounts.webp)

## 4. Tap Add to storage

When complete, your item is listed on the **Storage** page with the servings left and when to use it by.

![The Storage page lists Sweet Potato Lentil Patties in the Freezer, 8 of 8 servings left, Use within 90d.](/guides/storage-4-saved.webp)
