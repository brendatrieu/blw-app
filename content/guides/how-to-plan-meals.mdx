Planning is optional. Plan one meal, a whole week, or nothing, and log meals as usual whenever you like. A plan never logs itself: a meal counts once you serve it.

## 1. Pick a day

Tap a day in the week strip. The arrows change the week.

![The week strip, Sunday to Saturday, with Friday the 20th picked and today (Thursday the 19th) outlined. Friday's Breakfast has Oats and Pear; Lunch, Snack, and Dinner read Nothing planned yet.](/guides/plan-1-day.webp)

## 2. Plan a meal

Each day starts with your daily meals. Tap one to plan it, or tap **+ Add a meal** for another. Tap **+ Add more** to fill the plate, add a time if you like, then tap **Save**. If a container may not keep until that day, a note says so. It's only a heads-up; you can still save.

![Plan a meal for Dinner on Sat, Nov 21 at 5:30 PM. On the plate are Sweet Potato Lentil Patties from the Freezer and Pear from the Fridge with the note "May not keep until this day.", and Save and Save & copy to… sit at the bottom.](/guides/plan-2-meal.webp)

## 3. Copy it to other days (optional)

Tap **Save & copy to…** instead of **Save**, or pick **Copy to…** from a planned meal's ⋮ menu. Tap the days you want. Each day picks its meal with the same name, and you can change that under **Meals**. If one already has something planned, choose **Keep it** or **Replace it**. Then tap **Copy**.

![Copy Lunch, from Thu, Nov 19: today (Thursday) is outlined, and Friday and Saturday are picked, each with a check. Under Meals, "Sat 21 already has a Lunch planned." shows Keep it and Replace it, and Copy is at the bottom.](/guides/plan-3-copy.webp)

## 4. Serve it

When a planned meal's day comes, it shows **Serve**. Today, one tap logs it just as planned, and Home's **Up next** has the same **Serve**. For an earlier day, or if something ran out, **Serve** opens the meal so you can check it first. Once served, rate each item or tap **Add more**.

![Today's Breakfast reads Served 8:05 AM, with stars on each food, and Lunch below it has a Serve button.](/guides/plan-4-serve.webp)

## 5. Set your daily meals (optional)

Tap **Daily meals** at the top to choose the meals every new day starts with. Rename them, tap **+ Add a meal**, drag to reorder, and add times if you like, then tap **Save**. Days you've already planned keep their meals.

![Daily meals lists Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, and Dinner, each with a drag handle and a time, with + Add a meal and Save below.](/guides/plan-5-daily.webp)
